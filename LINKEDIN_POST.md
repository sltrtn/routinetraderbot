# LinkedIn Post

Built a small personal tool that watches the Indian market for me: RoutineTraderBot.

It runs three engines — pre-market IPO verdicts, intraday catalyst alerts, and a post-market filings digest — and pings me on Telegram. Everything is zero-cost: Python, SQLite, Gemini free tier, Telegram bot API, and an Oracle Cloud free VM.

The part I cared about most was credibility. Every alert needs either one regulatory source (NSE/BSE/SEBI) or two independent news sources before it fires. No signal is sent on hype alone.

Code is open if you want to look, fork, or tell me what I got wrong.

https://github.com/sltrtn/routinetraderbot
